## Narrative thread (phase 2, audit §4)
Through-line, asked by the film and answered by the conclusion: **What evidence would make us trust a model's prediction enough to use it?**
Each section in `presentation/sections.ts` carries its audience `question`, the one-sentence `takeaway` (its answer), a `bridge`
(the reason for the next section, as a question), a planned `minutes` budget and a `track` (core / appendix). The last step of
every core section shows the answer and the next question (`sections/common/SectionBridge.vue`, in the page flow, never an
overlay). The index shows the minutes; the core route is planned at **80 minutes**, to be validated in rehearsal.

| Block | Sections | Minutes | Question |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Opening | intro | 5 | Can a prediction be trusted? |
| AI, ML | ai, machine-learning | 7 | Which part is learned from examples? |
| The process | how-learning-works, section-8-2 | 10 | Where do predictions come from? |
| Choosing models | section-8-3 | 13 | Grouping, classifying, or predicting a number? |
| Regression worked example | worked-example-movies | 12 | How does a model learn a numerical prediction? |
| Evaluation | section-8-4 (with "back to the movies") | 12 | Does a good training fit work on unseen observations? |
| Deep learning | section-8-5 | 8 | What changes when the input is an image? |
| Data quality and ethics | section-8-6, ethics | 10 | Who or what does the model fail? |
| Resolution and recall | conclusion | 3 | What evidence is still missing? |
| Appendix | lab | — | exploration after the lecture |

Dataset changes are deliberate transfers, and the film is labelled a fictional scenario: no flower, movie, digit or penguin
result is presented as evidence about a clinical system.

## Transitions
- Intro → AI: the video's final frame is sampled into particles. They drift into the AI section's deterministic constellation (`visualizations/shared/ParticleField.ts`), so the first AI frame continues the last film frame.
- Between sections: the shared particle background stays alive in the shell; section content cross-fades (or cuts, in reduced motion). No slide wipes.
