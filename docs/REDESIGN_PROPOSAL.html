Part 2

The idea: one case, five acts

The film already gives us a story, a cast and a world. Today the sections after the film switch to a neutral "lecture" voice. The proposal is to keep that story running to the last slide: Noé investigates how RadiologyML works, and each section of Chapter 8 becomes one act of his investigation.

The cast stays on stage

2D versions of Noé and Dr. Salizar (same faces, hair and clothes as the film) appear on every step. Noé asks the question, which is the line we already show in amber, and Dr. Salizar answers. A set of 8 poses and 6 expressions covers all slides.

The film world as a living backdrop

The opening's 3D world can draw any moment of the film. Each act opens with a 4–6 second camera move inside it: the rainy hospital, the lab, the RadiologyML hall, the pixel field, the data landscape. Then it settles behind the slide, slowed and dimmed.

Bigger pictures, fewer words

Every step gets one hero visual that fills at least 60 % of the screen. Definitions move into "case file" cards that slide in after the picture. Small print (sources, notes) goes into a presenter-notes view.

Bilingual by design

All on-screen text lives in content files, so an EN / SQ switch is a data job, not a redesign. Albanian text needs one review by a native speaker in the department.
