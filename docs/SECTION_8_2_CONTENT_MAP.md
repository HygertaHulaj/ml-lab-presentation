## Source notes
- The book's RadiologyML wording ("learns from their corrections") is presented together with the film's responsible version: corrections are reviewed and validated before a new model version.
- 8.2 contains **no specific algorithms**. Decision trees, regression versus classification, inference versus prediction and interpretability are **§8.3**; validation, overfitting and "no free lunch" are **§8.4**; deep learning is **§8.5**. Algorithm simulations therefore belong to the Lab section (and later §8.3–8.4 modules), not to 8.2.
